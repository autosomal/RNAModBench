# Figure S8 legend (paste-ready for the Supporting Information layout)

**Figure S8. Replicate-resolved ncRNA metagene of the non-m6A detection tools in
HeLa wild type and unmodified in vitro transcribed RNA.** All profiles are
computed per independent sequencing unit (HeLa WT1–3 and unmodified HeLa
IVT rep1–3) on the Ensembl GRCh38.112 non-coding transcript model
(1 kb | ncRNA body | 1 kb); no m6A-centred reference is used, and the unmodified
IVT libraries are negative controls throughout. **(A)** Density of the sites
called by each of the six tools over the ncRNA metagene (one panel per tool);
blue, HeLa WT; orange, unmodified
HeLa IVT; solid filled curve, majority consensus (site called in ≥ 2 of 3
independent units); thin dashed curves, the individual units. **(B)** Divergence
between the WT and the unmodified-IVT profiles, per tool: open circles, the nine
independent WT × IVT unit pairs; red diamond, their mean; grey band, 2.5–97.5%
of a chromosome-stratified permutation null (R = 1000) that redraws as many
positions as the observed majority call set from each library's own
candidate-site universe inside the ncRNA model; dashed line, null median. Note
the logarithmic axis. **(C)** Change of the ncRNA-body share between the
conditions (WT − IVT) per unit pair; red diamond, majority consensus; dashed
line, no change. **(D)** Jensen–Shannon divergence (JSD, in bits) between each
condition's majority profile and the profile of its own candidate-site universe
inside the ncRNA model ("background"); the bands are the corresponding
permutation nulls (R = 1000; seed 20260919).

Tool names follow the nomenclature of the manuscript (CHEUI-m5C, NanoMUD-Ψ,
NanoMUD-m¹Ψ, NanoNm, NanoPsu, NanoSPA-Ψ).
