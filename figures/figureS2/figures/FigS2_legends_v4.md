## Caption (paste into the Supporting Information)

**Figure S2.** Detailed motif analysis of m6A detection tools and controls for tool-specific algorithmic bias (R3-6). (A) 5-mer sequence logos of the detected sites for the remaining 10 tools (mean KL divergence from the RRACH consensus next to each tool); the shared y-axis is the per-position information content in bits, computed on the replicate-equal-weight mean 5-mer probability matrix of each tool x species. (B) Top 5 preferred 5-mer motifs of each tool in four species; cell colour is the relative frequency of the 5-mer (shared scale, 0-0.45); RRACH 5-mers are shown in bold orange. (C) Top-5 5-mer identity across tools and species: the five most frequent 5-mers of two tools in the same species overlap far less (median 1 of 5, n = 289 tool pairs) than those of the same tool in two species (median 2 of 5, n = 72 pairs; Mann-Whitney one-sided p = 3.6e-06), i.e. motif identity follows the tool rather than the species. (D) Within-tool difference between the frequencies of AGAC-type and GGAC-type 5-mers: positive values indicate the plant-type AGAC motif, negative values the animal-type GGAC motif (Arabidopsis 12/13 tools positive; human 13/13 and mouse 11/13 negative). The direction is systematic but small: a two-way decomposition of the KL divergence assigns 89% of the variance to the tool factor versus 3% to the species factor (F = 23.9, p = 3.1e-08), so these motif differences characterise the tools rather than species biology.
