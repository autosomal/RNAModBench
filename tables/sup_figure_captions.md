# Captions for the supplementary figures (revision, 2026-09-21)

Style follows the main-text captions: one short lead sentence, then one clause per
panel; the candidate-site set and the unit conventions are stated once, in S1.

**Figure S1.** Metagene profiles of the detected m6A sites across mRNA regions, computed per independent sequencing unit. One panel per tool configuration and species, wild type (blue) versus the modification-deficient condition (orange; fip37 KD in Arabidopsis, Mettl3 KO in mouse, unmodified IVT in HeLa); thick lines are the majority consensus of a group's units and thin lines the individual units. Sites are assigned to the longest overlapping transcript and plotted with Guitar (1 kb upstream, 5'UTR, CDS, 3'UTR, 1 kb downstream).

**Figure S2.** Motif analysis of the detected sites for tool-specific algorithmic bias. (A) 5-mer sequence logos of the remaining 10 tools, with the mean KL divergence from the RRACH consensus beside each name; the y-axis is information content in bits. (B) Top-5 5-mers per tool and species, coloured by relative frequency (RRACH-conforming 5-mers in bold orange). (C) Shared 5-mers of two tools in one species versus the same tool in two species (medians 1 versus 2.5 of 5; Mann-Whitney one-sided P = 3.6 x 10-6). (D) AGAC-type minus GGAC-type 5-mer frequency per tool: positive in 12 of 13 Arabidopsis tools, negative in 13 of 13 human and 11 of 13 mouse tools. (E) Variance decomposition of (A): tool 89%, species 3%, residual 7% (F = 23.9, P = 3.1 x 10-8).

**Figure S3.** GLORI reference construction and tool performance against it. (A) Overlap of the two GLORI biological replicates per species; the high-confidence reference is the replicate-overlapping set with modification ratio > 0.1 (Arabidopsis 80,624; mouse 41,961; human 112,451 sites). (B) PPV against GLORI within the candidate-site set at the 2-bp window, per independent unit (Arabidopsis and HeLa n = 3; mouse, its two studies); bar, group mean; error bar, SD. (C) Tool ratio versus GLORI ratio at shared sites, with per-replicate Pearson r and Lin's CCC printed for each tool.

**Figure S4.** Purified sites and the matching-window trade-off. (A) Calls of every tool before and after purification (wild-type calls absent from the deficient sample), per independent unit. (B) PPV against GLORI and (C) the exact-localisation fraction of the matched calls as a function of the matching window (0-50 bp), on a shared x-axis; each line is a group mean, the dark band its bootstrap 95% CI and the pale band the individual units. (D) PPV against GLORI before and after purification; the filter selects on each tool's own calls, so the subset is not an independent accuracy estimate.

**Figure S5.** Independent characterisation of the purified-site definition, over the three site groups of every wild-type/deficient pair (def-only, shared, purified; Arabidopsis and HeLa n = 3 pairs, mouse one pair per study). (A) Overlap with the GLORI reference (2 bp). (B) DRACH fraction against the fraction of each sample's own candidate-site set (dashed). (C) Stoichiometry-semantic score of purified versus shared sites; the direction of the difference between the two groups varies from tool to tool.

**Figure S6.** Beyond the five selected tools. (A) Greedy path from the best single tool to all 13 configurations: union recall, union PPV, intersection PPV and the recall cost of intersecting. (B) Coverage of each configuration's calls and (C) their DRACH fraction, one dot per unit. (D) False-positive burden of every configuration on both unmodified controls (filled, Curlcake IVT; open, Human IVT; per 10 kb). (E) DRACH fraction of the site sets behind the trade-off (best single tool, both intersections, the sites the union adds, the GLORI reference).

**Figure S7.** Non-m6A detection per independent sequencing unit. (A) False-positive burden of the five tools run on the unmodified Curlcakes, per construct. (B) Overlap within a condition versus between conditions for every replicate pair. (C) Score range of each unit (median and IQR), the last panel of the figure; the depth-standardised call count of each condition and the coverage-matched IVT-to-WT ratio are tabulated in Table S5, the raw per-unit counts and unions being deposited with the call sets (the ratio is also drawn per unit in Fig. 7A), and the cross-check against the per-site Nm stoichiometry published with the model, which is one pooled run with no unmodified control of its own, is quoted in the manuscript text rather than drawn here.

**Figure S8.** Replicate-resolved non-coding metagene of the non-m6A tools. (A) Density of each tool's calls over the ncRNA model, one panel per tool (blue, HeLa WT; orange, unmodified IVT; solid, majority consensus; thin dashed, individual units). (B) WT-versus-IVT divergence per replicate pair against a chromosome-stratified permutation null (R = 1000). (C) Change of the ncRNA-body share per pair. (D) Jensen-Shannon divergence of each condition against its own candidate-site set.

**Figure S9.** Detection counts, explicit false-positive control, window-resolved accuracy and calibration of the RNA004 calling. (A) Detected sites per model in HeLa wild type (blue) and unmodified IVT (orange); an open circle on the axis floor marks a measured zero and an open left triangle an entry never measured on that library; the rows hold the eight Dorado m6A models, the five other m6A tools and the ten models for other modification types. (B) The eight ORCA channels on the same libraries, which ORCA reports alongside m6A. (C) False positives per 10^6 candidate adenosines on the unmodified RNA004 Curlcake control, with the matching specificity on the top axis. (D) Positive predictive value against GLORI and (E) the exact-localisation fraction against the matching window (0-50 bp) for all thirteen entries, each named in the key of the sweep printed under the two sweep cells. (F) Pearson r against GLORI and (G) the least-squares calibration slope (filled dot) and Lin's concordance correlation coefficient (CCC, open dot) of every ratio tool, each with its 95 % interval. (H) Rank stability of the tool ordering against transcript coverage: the Spearman rho of the 13-tool ranking recomputed at the coverage floors >= 5 and >= 20 reads and inside the informative coverage strata (20-49 and >= 50 reads) against the primary ranking, one marker per species (Arabidopsis circle, mouse square, HeLa triangle; the species key of those two cells prints under each of their boxes). (I) The same statistic against the reference stratified by its own modification ratio, one row per stratum (ratio 0.1-0.3, 0.3-0.6 and > 0.6).

**Figure S10.** Built-in Dorado models for the other modification types. (A) Metagene profiles of the six pseudouridine and m5C models across the mRNA regions, plotted with Guitar (1 kb upstream, 5'UTR, CDS, 3'UTR, 1 kb downstream), in HeLa wild type and unmodified IVT; the wild-type/unmodified-IVT key is repeated in each panel. (B) False-positive density of the same models on the unmodified synthetic control as a function of the modified-read threshold. (C) Score validity in HeLa: Mann-Whitney AUC of each model's own modified fraction between wild type and unmodified IVT.
