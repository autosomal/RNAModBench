# Captions for the supplementary figures (revision, 2026-09-21)

Style follows the main-text captions: one short lead sentence, then one clause per
panel; the candidate-site set and the unit conventions are stated once, in S1.

**Figure S1.** Metagene distribution of the detected m6A sites across mRNA regions, computed per independent sequencing unit. One panel per tool configuration and species, wild type (blue) versus the modification-deficient condition (orange; fip37 KD in Arabidopsis, Mettl3 KO in mouse, unmodified IVT in HeLa); thick lines are the majority consensus of a group's units and thin lines the individual units. Sites are assigned to the longest overlapping transcript and plotted with Guitar (1 kb upstream, 5'UTR, CDS, 3'UTR, 1 kb downstream).

**Figure S2.** Motif analysis of the detected sites for tool-specific algorithmic bias. (A) 5-mer sequence logos of the remaining 10 tools, with the mean KL divergence from the RRACH consensus beside each name; the y-axis is information content in bits. (B) Top-5 5-mers per tool and species, coloured by relative frequency (RRACH-conforming 5-mers in bold orange). (C) Shared 5-mers of two tools in one species versus the same tool in two species (medians 1 versus 2.5 of 5; Mann-Whitney one-sided P = 3.6 x 10-6). (D) AGAC-type minus GGAC-type 5-mer frequency per tool: positive in 12 of 13 Arabidopsis tools, negative in 13 of 13 human and 11 of 13 mouse tools. (E) Variance decomposition of (A): tool 89%, species 3%, residual 7% (F = 23.9, P = 3.1 x 10-8).

**Figure S3.** GLORI reference construction and tool performance against it. (A) Overlap of the two GLORI biological replicates per species; the high-confidence reference is the replicate-overlapping set with modification ratio > 0.1 (Arabidopsis 80,624; mouse 41,961; human 112,451 sites). (B) PPV against GLORI within the candidate-site set at the 2-bp window, per independent unit (Arabidopsis and HeLa n = 3; mouse, its two studies, never pooled); bar, group mean; error bar, SD. (C) Tool ratio versus GLORI ratio at shared sites, with per-replicate Pearson r and Lin's CCC printed for each tool.

**Figure S4.** Purified sites and the matching-window trade-off. (A) Calls of every tool before and after purification (wild-type calls absent from the deficient sample), per independent unit. (B) PPV against GLORI and (C) the exact-nucleotide fraction of the matched calls as a function of the matching window (0-50 bp), on a shared x-axis; each line is a group mean, the dark band its bootstrap 95% CI and the pale band the individual units. (D) PPV against GLORI before and after purification; the filter selects on each tool's own calls, so the subset is descriptive only.

**Figure S5.** Beyond the five selected tools. (A) Greedy path from the best single tool to all 13 configurations: union recall, union PPV, intersection PPV and the recall cost of intersecting. (B) Coverage of each configuration's calls and (C) their DRACH fraction, one dot per unit. (D) False-positive burden of every configuration on both unmodified controls (filled, Curlcake IVT; open, HeLa IVT; per 10 kb). (E) DRACH fraction of the site sets behind the trade-off (best single tool, the sites the union adds, both intersections, the GLORI reference).

**Figure S6.** Non-m6A detection per independent sequencing unit. (A) False-positive burden of the five tools run on the unmodified Curlcakes, per construct. (B) Overlap within a condition versus between conditions for every replicate pair. (C) Score range of each unit (median and IQR). (D) Calls per unit in HeLa wild type and unmodified IVT with the IVT/WT ratio, including the union ratios tabulated in Table S5.

**Figure S7.** Replicate-resolved non-coding metagene of the non-m6A tools. (A) Density of each tool's calls over the ncRNA model, one panel per tool (blue, HeLa WT; orange, unmodified IVT; solid, majority consensus; thin dashed, individual units). (B) WT-versus-IVT divergence per replicate pair against a chromosome-stratified permutation null (R = 1000). (C) Change of the ncRNA-body share per pair. (D) Jensen-Shannon divergence of each condition against its own candidate-site set.

**Figure S8.** Detection counts, false-positive control, window-resolved accuracy and effect size of the RNA004 calling. (A) Detected sites per model in HeLa wild type (blue) and unmodified IVT (orange); an open triangle marks a model without a call. (B) The eight ORCA channels on the same libraries. (C) False positives per 10^6 candidate adenosines on the unmodified RNA004 Curlcake control with the matching specificity axis. (D) Matching window versus PPV (upper) and exact-nucleotide fraction (lower) for every entry measured on the RNA004 library. (E) Pearson r against GLORI per ratio tool with its Fisher-z 95% interval. (F) Effect size of the same tools (OLS slope of the tool ratio on the GLORI ratio with its bootstrap 95% interval; dashed line, 1.0).

**Figure S9.** Built-in Dorado models for the other modification types. (A) Guitar density of the six pseudouridine and m5C models across the mRNA model, each panel with its own key, in HeLa wild type and unmodified IVT. (B) False-positive density of the same models on the unmodified synthetic control as a function of the modified-read threshold. (C) Score validity in HeLa: Mann-Whitney AUC of each model's own modified fraction between wild type and unmodified IVT.

**Figure S10.** Independent characterisation of the purified-site definition, over the three site groups of every wild-type/deficient pair (def-only, shared, purified; Arabidopsis and HeLa n = 3 pairs, mouse one pair per study). (A) Overlap with the GLORI reference (2 bp). (B) DRACH fraction against the fraction of each sample's own candidate-site set (dashed). (C) Stoichiometry-semantic score of purified versus shared sites; the direction is tool-dependent, so the panel is read descriptively.
